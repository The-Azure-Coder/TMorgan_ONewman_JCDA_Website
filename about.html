About JCDA

Jamaica Community Development Association is a community-focused organization working alongside residents, schools, and local partners across Jamaica.

Our work turns community ideas into practical projects that support young people, strengthen families, protect the environment, and improve shared spaces.

Explore Our Projects

Our Mission

To empower communities through education, youth development, environmental projects, and meaningful outreach.

Our Vision

Stronger, healthier Jamaican communities where people have the support and opportunity to thrive.

What Guides Us
